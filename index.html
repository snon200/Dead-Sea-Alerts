UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Alignment

Dead Sea Alerts uses NASA-ISRO NISAR L-band InSAR to detect ground subsidence early and show which infrastructure is exposed. It supports four UN SDG targets:

SDG 11 · Target 11.2 — Safe & sustainable transportFlags subsidence along Route 90 and bridges, helping keep the main transport corridor safe for everyone using it.
SDG 11 · Target 11.5 — Reduce disaster lossesEarly detection of 100–1,000 m subsidence bowls before sinkhole collapse helps reduce casualties and economic losses.
SDG 13 · Target 13.1 — Resilience to climate-related hazardsContinuous radar monitoring strengthens adaptive capacity to water-level-driven geological hazards.
SDG 13 · Target 13.3 — Awareness & early warningAn open, easy-to-read dashboard (with accessibility options) builds awareness and institutional capacity for early warning.
